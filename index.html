About PHSS Alumni Community

This is the official website of Pabiacherra Class XII School alumni association. Our primary focus is connecting alumni, supporting the school, promoting personal and professional development, and support the mission and goals of our alma mater. We celebrate the diverse perspectives of our alumni by organizing events that foster intellectual dialogue and promote lifelong learning across various disciplines. We are committed to providing our members with valuable resources and opportunities to connect, learn, and grow. As a community we celebrate achievements, social responsibities and clean & green environment. Together we are creating a better world for future.

Silver Jubilee Reunion of Class 1999

Pabiacherra Class XII School, class of 1999 has celebrated their "Silver Jubilee Reunion" on November 16, 2024. The one day event full of activities took place at Kumarghat Geetanjali Auditorium. The night was celebrated with friends and families.
